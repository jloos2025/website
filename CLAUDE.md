# Claude instructions

The full working guide for this repository is in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Read it
before making changes. It covers the writing rules, the site structure and why it
is built that way, and what not to change.

Architecture and deployment: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

Two rules worth repeating here because they are absolute:

1. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, in any file, including commit messages.
2. Never invent facts about the business. No founding year, no certifications,
   no response time promises, no customer testimonials that did not come from a
   real customer.
